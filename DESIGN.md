# Design System: Tremplin — Campus Wayfinder

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Campus Wayfinder"**

Tremplin feels like a useful public-interest guide on a Moroccan campus: limestone paper, midnight ink, Majorelle-blue signals, compact geometry, numbered index cues, and flat category posters. The implemented direction is the grounded **candidate 4, seed `b9ab74ff`**. It rejects the centered marketing hero and interchangeable image-card wall in favor of an asymmetric catalogue entrance that puts live scope, search, filters, facts, and source continuation first.

The interface is calm but firm. Borders and ruled dividers organize dense information; small blue bars and index labels provide orientation. Real listings—not lifestyle imagery—carry the experience.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Asymmetric, catalogue-first composition with search as the primary first-viewport action.
- Flat poster art for every opportunity type; source logos are optional overlays, never structural dependencies.
- Compact radii, visible rules, logical-direction spacing, and restrained responsive motion.
- French, Arabic, and English are equal design cases across light and dark themes.

## Colors

The light theme pairs warm limestone neutrals with cool midnight ink; Majorelle blue is the navigation and action signal. Dark mode remaps the same semantic roles rather than merely inverting the page.

### Primary

- **Majorelle signal:** Use `majorelle` for primary actions, active controls, index bars, focus identity, and sparse orientation marks. Use its hover, soft, foreground, and ring companions by semantic role.

### Secondary

- **Deadline signals:** `urgent`, `soon`, and `calm` each pair with a soft background. They communicate timing/status only; they are not decorative accents.
- **Limestone secondary:** Use the neutral secondary surface for quiet hover, ghost, and icon treatments.

### Neutral

- **Limestone field:** `limestone` is the page ground, `paper` the raised reading surface, and `limestone-sunken` the recessed/filter-detail surface.
- **Midnight ink:** `midnight-ink` carries primary text; muted and subtle variants carry supporting facts and labels. Border, strong-border, and input tokens establish hierarchy without heavy boxes.

### Category Poster System

Every category has a stable surface/ink/accent triad plus dark surface and ink tokens. Keep these assignments fixed:

| Type | Marker | Icon | Token prefix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job | `JOB` | Briefcase | `poster-job-*` |
| Internship | `STAGE` | Rocket | `poster-internship-*` |
| Bachelor/Licence | `BAC+3` | Book with check | `poster-bachelor-*` |
| Master | `BAC+5` | Graduation cap | `poster-master-*` |
| Doctorate | `PHD` | Microscope | `poster-doctorat-*` |
| Scholarship | `BOURSE` | Sparkles | `poster-scholarship-*` |
| Concours | `CONCOURS` | Trophy | `poster-concours-*` |

Each poster includes an accent rail at logical start, crosshair rules, a stable ID-derived two-digit index, a small uppercase marker, and a centered square icon tile. A source logo, when present, sits on a white plate above the complete poster; a failed logo disappears cleanly.

**The Signal Rarity Rule.** Majorelle blue marks action, selection, or orientation. Do not wash large general-purpose surfaces in it.

**The Status Semantics Rule.** Urgent, soon, and calm colors must continue to mean deadline state.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Readex Pro (Latin and Arabic subsets; weights 400–700)
**Body Font:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic (Latin and Arabic subsets; weights 400–600)

**Character:** Readex Pro gives headings and numeric indices a compact wayfinding voice; IBM Plex Sans Arabic keeps mixed-script facts and controls readable. Both cover Latin and Arabic so a French institution and Arabic title can coexist without mid-line fallback.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** The home title uses the `display` token and stays within roughly 14 characters per line; detail titles use `headline` at large screens and 1.875rem on small screens.
- **Title:** Section and card headings use `title`, usually at 1–1.125rem with a snug line height.
- **Body:** Dense catalogue copy defaults to `body`; supporting lead copy grows to 1–1.125rem with relaxed leading.
- **Label:** Indices, filter-group labels, and metadata use `label`, uppercase in Latin contexts. Keep figures tabular in stats, counts, and pagination.

Arabic pages remove negative heading tracking and use a 1.5 heading line height; body leading becomes 1.75. Never force Latin tracking or uppercase behavior onto Arabic content.

**The Mixed-Script Rule.** Keep Readex Pro and IBM Plex Sans Arabic as the paired families; do not introduce a Latin-only display face.

## Layout

The shared canvas is at least 320px wide. Primary pages use a centered 80rem container with 16px mobile gutters and 24px gutters from 640px upward. The base rhythm concentrates on 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, and 48px; use 20px gaps for repeated card grids.

The home first viewport is a bordered surface: an asymmetric title field, live three-item index, and full-width search/filter panel along its lower edge. From 1024px, title and stats become a 1.35fr/0.65fr split and the stats stack vertically; below that, stats form a three-column strip. Opportunity cards stack poster above copy below 640px, become a 176px poster/content split at 640px, and remain one-up until a two-column grid at 1280px.

Detail pages stack on small screens. From 1024px, the poster/title header becomes a 0.78fr/1.22fr split; body content pairs with a fixed 23rem action rail whose apply card sticks 96px from the top. Logical properties (`start`, `end`, `margin-inline-start`, `padding-inline-*`) govern alignment. Data strings use `dir="auto"`; directional arrows and micro-translations mirror in RTL.

**The Search-First Rule.** Search and the visible type chips belong in the first viewport; advanced filters reveal from that same surface.

**The Logical-Edge Rule.** New layouts must work by direction, not by hardcoded left/right assumptions.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat and ruled by default, with restrained shadows only on actionable or focal surfaces. `shadow-card` is `0 1px 0 oklch(0.19 0.036 263 / 0.05), 0 12px 30px -24px oklch(0.19 0.07 263 / 0.34)`; `shadow-lift` is `0 1px 0 oklch(0.19 0.036 263 / 0.07), 0 20px 45px -26px oklch(0.19 0.07 263 / 0.42)`. Dark mode deepens these ambient shadows. Poster icon tiles and the brand mark use deliberate hard-offset shadows (5px and 3px) as graphic registration marks, not as a general elevation style.

Cards lift 2px and change from card shadow to lift shadow on fine-pointer hover, then compress to 0.985 on press. State transitions run for 180–200ms; surface entrances run for 420ms with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` and may stagger by 80–140ms. Reduced-motion mode disables smooth scrolling and collapses animations/transitions to effectively zero.

**The Ruled-First Rule.** Establish structure with borders, tonal layers, and accent rails before adding shadow.

## Shapes

Controls use gently compact 10px corners; brand marks and tiny counters use 8px; cards use 12px; empty states may use 16px. Full pills are reserved for the language switcher and truly circular controls. Major wayfinding panels—the filter surface, detail hero, content sections, fact tiles, and apply rail—stay square or nearly square, often with a 1px rule or 4px blue edge. Poster icon tiles are square with a 2px outline.

**The Compact Geometry Rule.** Do not round every container into a soft floating capsule; square ruled modules are a defining counterpoint.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Shape:** 10px corners, semibold 14px label, 40px default height; large actions are 48px high.
- **Primary:** Majorelle fill with its high-contrast foreground; use for sign-in, profile completion, and apply/source continuation.
- **Outline / Ghost / Link:** Paper plus strong rule; quiet neutral hover; or text-only Majorelle with underline on hover.
- **States:** 200ms color/transform transition, 0.98 active scale, 2px focus ring with background offset, and 50% opacity with no pointer events when disabled.

### Chips and Badges

- **Filter chips:** 36px minimum height, 10px corners, 12px horizontal padding. Unselected chips use paper/muted text and a border; selected chips invert to Majorelle. Preserve `aria-pressed`.
- **Badges:** 11px semibold type, 8px corners, 8px/4px padding. Use soft Majorelle for type, solid Majorelle for “new,” semantic deadline pairs for urgency, and bordered paper for domain metadata.
- **Language switcher:** The intentional pill exception; the active locale uses Majorelle and `aria-current` while the route and query state are preserved.

### Cards and Containers

- **Opportunity card:** 12px clipped corners, paper surface, 1px rule, restrained shadow, poster/content split, and a full-card stretched title link. Hover lifts only on fine pointers; closed listings reduce to 70% opacity.
- **Filter panel:** Square paper module with strong border, card shadow, and a 4px logical-start Majorelle rail. Expanded filters enter below a rule on a sunken surface.
- **Detail section:** Square bordered paper with a short Majorelle title bar. The apply rail uses a 4px top signal and keeps the original-source action dominant.

### Inputs and Fields

Search and selects are 48px high; standard profile fields are 44px. They use the limestone ground, 10px corners, a 1px input rule, and a 2px Majorelle focus ring. Search reserves 40px on both inline edges for its icon and pending spinner. Labels may be visually hidden but must remain programmatically associated.

### Navigation

The header is a sticky 68px paper bar with a subtle backdrop blur and bottom rule. Its brand mark uses a hard-offset midnight shadow. Secondary navigation is muted at rest, gains a neutral background on hover, and keeps a visible focus ring. Text labels progressively hide on compact widths; icon-only controls retain accessible names.

### States

- **Loading:** Opportunity-shaped skeletons preserve card geometry; filter search shows an end-positioned spinner.
- **Empty/error:** Distinguish not configured, load error, empty database, and filtered no-match states. Use a dashed strong rule, neutral icon medallion, concise explanation, and a recovery action when possible.
- **Save:** Bookmarking updates optimistically, exposes `aria-pressed`, disables while pending, and routes signed-out users to login.
- **Missing media:** The category poster is always complete; a failed institution logo removes itself without leaving a blank plate.
- **Theme:** Support explicit light/dark choice plus system preference. Components must consume semantic tokens, while posters use their paired category theme tokens.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** preserve the Campus Wayfinder contract and provenance seed `b9ab74ff` when extending the world.
- **Do** use real catalogue facts and source-provided logos; treat scraped content as information, not promotional proof.
- **Do** keep search, filtering, saving, applying, pagination, and profile controls keyboard accessible with visible focus and WCAG AA normal-text contrast.
- **Do** use semantic elements and state attributes: links for navigation, buttons for actions, labels for fields, and `aria-expanded`, `aria-pressed`, or `aria-current` where applicable.
- **Do** verify French, Arabic, English, RTL, dark mode, missing media, loading, empty, error, pending, disabled, closed, hover, focus, and reduced-motion states.

### Don't:

- **Don't** introduce a centered marketing hero, generic photo wall, fabricated statistics, institutions, testimonials, outcomes, or official-status implications.
- **Don't** replace complete category posters with blank placeholders when imagery is missing.
- **Don't** use urgency colors for decoration, or Majorelle as a broad background wash.
- **Don't** hardcode physical left/right layout for directional content, or let icons/text animations move the wrong way in RTL.
- **Don't** hide the original-source link or the verification disclaimer; Tremplin guides candidates to evidence rather than presenting scraped text as authority.
