What lives down there, and what this is

The exchange

Anything bigger than a rat draws back before it swings, and the tiles it is about to cover turn red for one turn. That turn is yours. Step out of them and the blow hits nothing. Guard (g) and most of it goes into your guard. Bash (b) and the swing comes apart before it lands.

Guarding a blow, and striking something that is already drawing back, earn footing — the dots beside your level. Footing pays for a bash, or for a heavy swing (H) that lands for double and leaves you wide open until your next turn.

Hit anything enough and it loses its footing instead: a reeling creature drops whatever it was winding up and misses two turns. Strike something that has not seen you and you do triple damage. Let two of them get either side of you and they all hit harder.

About

Every sprite here is drawn in code, pixel by pixel — there are no image files. The rules come from a terminal roguelike written in Python: the same room generation, the same field of view, the same combat arithmetic and balance figures.

Seeds do not match the terminal build, which draws from Python's random number generator. Your run is kept in this browser only; saving suspends a run, loading consumes the save, and dying deletes it.
