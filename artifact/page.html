What to know

Parry is the whole of it. Every enemy blow is telegraphed by a red flare before it lands. Guard on that flare and you take no damage, break the attacker open, and earn a riposte — your next strike does triple.

The Warden's shield turns aside ordinary cuts. Parry its swing, break the guard with a heavy, or roll behind it.

Death leaves your guilt where you fell, along with your tears, and binds part of your fervour until you walk back and reclaim it. Praying at an altar restores you — and wakes every hollow thing in the room.

The way to the Abbot is sealed. The second breath is held in the drowned cistern; the seal breaker waits above the gallery, and only a doubled jump will carry you there.
